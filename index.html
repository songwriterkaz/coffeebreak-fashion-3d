How printing works: this app gets the file ready and opens it the easy way. For a 3D printer it saves an STL file and a settings card, then you open the STL in your printer's own app (Bambu Studio, Creality Print or PrusaSlicer) and press print there. For a label printer it saves a PNG and PDF at the exact tape size to open in Brother P-touch Editor, iPrint&Label or DYMO software. For paper it opens your browser's print box. It does not control USB printers directly.

Easy steps

  1. Pick a design (bag, shoe, earring, charm, pattern, bow) or draw your own.
  2. Press See it in 3D to check it.
  3. Press Print and choose 3D printer, label printer or paper.
  4. 3D printer: open the saved STL in your printer app and print. Lay it flat, label side up.
  5. 3D pen: print the paper stencil, lay baking paper on top and trace.
  6. Labels: open the label file in your label printer app. Iron-on fabric tape can be ironed or sewn into clothes.

3D pens and printer nozzles get very hot. Adults should supervise children. PCL low-temperature pens are best for kids.
